# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Deal Room Dashboard is the browser-based operator console for the Kiro AI system, which sells AI-generated websites to local businesses for prices between $550 and $1,000. This spec covers the dashboard only. The discovery, qualification, site-generation, email, and calling automation (the "bot") will be specified separately.

The dashboard is the operator's single workspace: a filterable list of leads and deals, a per-deal "Deal Room" detail view, enforcement of the sales pipeline state machine, triggering of outreach and money actions, a human review gate for AI-generated sites, a payment-verification gate for website release, funnel and A/B analytics, and an audit trail of every operator action.

The system is operator-in-the-loop by design. The dashboard suggests prices, next actions, and send timing, and the operator makes every final decision. No action that moves money or delivers a website executes without explicit operator confirmation.

Because the automation components do not exist yet, the dashboard interacts with them through a Pipeline_Adapter boundary whose operations may be fulfilled by stub implementations. All state, gating, validation, and audit behavior specified here applies whether the adapter is backed by stubs or by the real pipeline.

## Glossary

- **Deal_Room_Dashboard**: The complete web application specified by this document, including its server-side components.
- **Operator**: An authenticated human user who reviews leads and executes sales actions in the Deal_Room_Dashboard.
- **Lead**: A prospective business record stored in the `leads` table, containing company_name, industry, website_url, contact_name, contact_email, contact_phone, owner, researched_score, preferred_price, status, and created_at.
- **Deal**: A commercial record stored in the `deals` table, containing deal_id, lead_id, agreed_price, quote_sent_date, invoice_id, payment_received, paid_date, delivery_sent, and delivered_date.
- **Site_Project**: A record of an AI-generated website preview for one Lead, containing preview_url, page_count, review_state which holds a Site_Review_State value, created_at, and generated_at.
- **Site_Review_State**: One of the Site_Project review_state values Generating, Ready_For_Review, Approved, Rejected.
- **Pipeline_State**: One of the values New_Lead, Contacted, Replied, Scheduled, Quoted, Won, Invoiced, Paid_Pending_Verification, Payment_Verified, Released, Closed_Lost.
- **Legal_Transition**: An ordered pair of Pipeline_State values listed in the transition table of Requirement 4.
- **Terminal_State**: The Pipeline_State values Released and Closed_Lost.
- **Pipeline_State_Machine**: The dashboard component that validates and applies Pipeline_State changes.
- **Lead_List_View**: The dashboard screen that lists Lead records with filtering, search, sorting, and bulk selection.
- **Deal_Room_View**: The dashboard screen that shows one Lead, its Deal, its Site_Project, its outreach history, and its available actions.
- **Outreach_Controller**: The dashboard component that submits outreach requests (prospect email, delivery email, logged call) to the Pipeline_Adapter.
- **Compliance_Guard**: The dashboard component that evaluates unsubscribed, do_not_call, bounced, duplicate, and calling-hours conditions before an outreach action is submitted.
- **Manual_Review_Flag**: The boolean stored on a Lead that the Compliance_Guard sets when a bounce is recorded against the Lead's contact_email.
- **Clearance_Timestamp**: The instant at which the Compliance_Guard evaluated one specific Operator-confirmed outreach action and found no blocking condition, recorded on that action's outreach request record and carried unchanged onto the email row or call row recorded for that action.
- **Late_Opt_Out_Marker**: The boolean stored on an email row or a call row that is set when the Pipeline_Adapter returned a success result for that outreach submission and the Lead's unsubscribed_at or do_not_call_at value was recorded after that submission's Clearance_Timestamp but before the row was written, marking the row as having been sent before the opt-out was processed.
- **Site_Review_Gate**: The dashboard component that requires Operator approval of a Site_Project before a preview link is emailed to a Lead.
- **Pricing_Advisor**: The dashboard component that computes a Suggested_Price from Lead attributes.
- **Suggested_Price**: The price in whole US dollars computed by the Pricing_Advisor as `min(1000, 550 + 150 * max(0, page_count - 3) + 150 * [website_condition <= 2] + 100 * [urgency >= 4])`.
- **Price_Floor**: 550 US dollars.
- **Price_Anchor**: 850 US dollars.
- **Price_Cap**: 1000 US dollars.
- **Invoice_Manager**: The dashboard component that creates and displays invoice records.
- **Payment_Verifier**: The dashboard component that records an Operator's confirmation that a received payment matches the Deal agreed_price.
- **Payment_Verified_Flag**: The boolean stored on a Deal that is set only by the Payment_Verifier, backed by the Deal's payment verification timestamp so that the flag reads as set exactly when that timestamp is set, and consulted by the Release_Gate as the authoritative record of payment verification.
- **Payment_Anomaly_Flag**: The boolean stored on a Deal that is set when a payment recorded under Requirement 8.3 could not be accompanied by the Paid_Pending_Verification Pipeline_State change, marking the Deal as requiring Operator attention, accompanied by a recorded anomaly reason, and cleared only by the explicit audited Operator action of Requirement 8.22.
- **Release_Gate**: The dashboard component that authorizes delivery of the finished website to a Lead.
- **Release_Authorization**: The record created when the Release_Gate accepts an Operator approval, containing deal_id, operator_id, and authorized_at.
- **Notification_Service**: The dashboard component that delivers event notifications to Operators by email and Slack.
- **Notification_Subscription**: The per-Operator, per-event-type setting that determines whether the Notification_Service generates a notification record for that Operator when an event of that type is accepted.
- **Channel_Delivery_Setting**: The per-Operator, per-event-type, per-channel setting that determines whether the Notification_Service attempts delivery of an already-generated notification on that channel, where the channels are Slack and email and the in-dashboard notification list is not a channel.
- **Analytics_View**: The dashboard screen that displays funnel counts, conversion rates, revenue figures, and A/B variant comparisons.
- **Reached_Count**: For a Pipeline_State and a date range, the number of Leads whose recorded Pipeline_State history contains that value with an occurrence timestamp inside the range.
- **Current_State_Count**: For a Pipeline_State and a date range, the number of Leads whose Pipeline_State equals that value and whose created_at falls inside the range.
- **Cohort**: For a date range, the set of Leads whose New_Lead entry in their recorded Pipeline_State history has an occurrence timestamp inside that range.
- **Cohort_Stage_Count**: For a Cohort and a Pipeline_State, the number of members of that Cohort whose recorded Pipeline_State history contains that value, counted without regard to whether that value was reached inside the Cohort's date range.
- **Reporting_Timezone**: The single timezone in which the Analytics_View interprets selected date range boundaries.
- **Variant**: A named A/B test arm applied to an outreach message, with dimensions subject_line, body_length, cta_style, send_timing, and price_anchor.
- **Audit_Logger**: The dashboard component that records every Operator action in an append-only log.
- **Audit_Entry**: A record containing actor_id, action_type, target_type, target_id, before_value, after_value, and occurred_at.
- **Auth_Service**: The dashboard component that authenticates Operators and authorizes actions.
- **Pipeline_Adapter**: The interface through which the Deal_Room_Dashboard requests automation work (site generation, email send, call logging, invoice creation) and receives events, backed by stub implementations until the automation components exist.
- **Idempotency_Key**: The identifier carried by every Pipeline_Adapter outbound operation, generated once per Operator-confirmed action and reused unchanged for every retry of that same confirmed action, where the outreach_request_id of Requirement 5.9 is the Idempotency_Key for the send prospect email and log outbound call operations.
- **Calling_Window**: The period from 08:00 to 20:00 in the Lead's local timezone.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Operator Authentication and Authorization

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want the Deal Room restricted to authenticated operators, so that lead data and money actions are protected.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an unauthenticated visitor requests any dashboard screen other than the sign-in screen, THE Auth_Service SHALL deny the request without displaying any Lead or Deal data, SHALL redirect the visitor to the sign-in screen, and SHALL retain the identifier of the requested screen for the duration of the sign-in attempt.
2. WHEN an Operator submits credentials that match a registered Operator account, THE Auth_Service SHALL establish a session carrying that Operator's operator_id and role, SHALL reset that account's consecutive-failure count to zero, SHALL display the retained requested screen when one was retained and the Operator's role permits that screen, SHALL display the Lead_List_View when no screen was retained, and SHALL complete the sign-in within 3 seconds.
3. IF an Operator submits credentials whose identifier is not registered or whose password does not match the registered account, THEN THE Auth_Service SHALL leave the session unestablished, SHALL display an authentication failure message that does not indicate which credential field failed, and SHALL increment that identifier's consecutive-failure count by one.
4. WHEN a session reaches 12 hours of age measured from the time the session was established, THE Auth_Service SHALL end the session and SHALL redirect the Operator's next request to the sign-in screen.
5. THE Auth_Service SHALL assign each Operator exactly one of the roles Viewer, Agent, or Admin, SHALL assign the Viewer role to every newly created Operator account, and SHALL apply a role change only when the change is submitted by an Operator holding the Admin role.
6. WHERE an Operator holds the Viewer role, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL display Lead and Deal data in read-only form, and IF that Operator submits a request to send outreach, create an invoice, verify a payment, authorize a release, edit a Lead field, manage Operator accounts, or configure a Variant, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the request, SHALL leave all Lead, Deal, invoice, payment, and Release_Authorization records unchanged, SHALL display an authorization failure message naming the role required for the action, and SHALL create an Audit_Entry recording the rejected attempt.
7. WHERE an Operator holds the Agent role, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL permit outreach, invoice creation, payment verification, and release authorization actions.
8. WHERE an Operator holds the Agent role, IF that Operator submits a request to manage Operator accounts or to configure a Variant, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the request, SHALL leave all Operator account and Variant records unchanged, SHALL display an authorization failure message naming the Admin role as required, and SHALL create an Audit_Entry recording the rejected attempt.
9. WHERE an Operator holds the Admin role, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL permit every action permitted to the Agent role and SHALL additionally permit Operator account management and Variant configuration.
10. FOR ALL action requests submitted by an Operator, including requests submitted without the corresponding dashboard control being displayed or enabled, THE Auth_Service SHALL evaluate session validity and the Operator's role before the action is applied, and SHALL reject the request when the session is absent, when the session has been ended, or when the Operator's role does not permit the action.
11. IF a sign-in attempt is submitted for an identifier whose consecutive-failure count has reached 5 within the preceding 15 minutes, THEN THE Auth_Service SHALL refuse every further sign-in attempt for that identifier until 15 minutes have elapsed from the fifth failure, SHALL leave the session unestablished, SHALL display the remaining refusal duration, and SHALL create an Audit_Entry recording the refused attempt.
12. WHEN a session reaches 30 continuous minutes during which no Operator request has been received, THE Auth_Service SHALL end the session and SHALL require re-authentication before any subsequent action request is applied.
13. WHEN an Operator activates the sign-out control, THE Auth_Service SHALL end the session within 2 seconds and SHALL display the sign-in screen.

### Requirement 2: Lead and Deal List View

**User Story:** As an operator, I want a filterable list of every lead and deal, so that I can find the work that needs attention now.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an authenticated Operator opens the Lead_List_View, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display for each Lead the company_name, industry, Pipeline_State, researched_score, agreed_price when a Deal exists, invoice status when an invoice exists, and the most-recent-activity timestamp computed as the latest of the Lead's email sent_at, opened_at, clicked_at, and reply_at values, call record timestamps, Pipeline_State change timestamps, and applied Operator action timestamps, where an applied Operator action is an Operator action the Deal_Room_Dashboard applied and where the timestamps of rejected action attempts are excluded from this source set.
2. WHEN an Operator selects one or more Pipeline_State filter values, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display only Lead records whose Pipeline_State is among the selected values, and WHEN no Pipeline_State filter value is selected, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display Lead records in every Pipeline_State.
3. WHEN an Operator enters a search term of 1 to 100 characters after leading and trailing whitespace is removed, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display Lead records whose company_name, contact_name, contact_email, or contact_phone contains that term as a substring, matched case-insensitively.
4. WHEN an Operator selects a sort column among company_name, industry, Pipeline_State, researched_score, agreed_price, and most-recent-activity timestamp, THE Lead_List_View SHALL order the displayed Lead records by that column in the selected ascending or descending direction, SHALL place records whose sort column has no value after all records that have a value, and SHALL break equal sort values by Lead id in ascending order.
5. WHILE more than 50 Lead records match the active filters, THE Lead_List_View SHALL paginate the results at 50 records per page and SHALL display the total match count, the current page number, and the total page count.
6. WHEN an Operator opens the Lead_List_View with a lead set of 5,000 records or fewer, THE Lead_List_View SHALL render the first page within 2 seconds of the request being submitted.
7. WHEN an Operator changes a filter, a search term, or a sort column, THE Lead_List_View SHALL update the displayed results within 1 second for a lead set of 5,000 records or fewer.
8. THE Lead_List_View SHALL display one distinct compliance badge per condition on each Lead row for each of the conditions unsubscribed set, do_not_call set, a bounced email, and a duplicate-contact flag, and each badge SHALL name the condition it represents.
9. WHEN an Operator selects a Lead row, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL open the Deal_Room_View for that Lead.
10. WHERE an action's Pipeline_State precondition defined in Requirements 4 through 8 is satisfied for a Lead and the Operator's role permits that action, THE Lead_List_View SHALL offer that action on the Lead row, and WHERE either condition is unsatisfied, THE Lead_List_View SHALL omit that action from the Lead row.
11. FOR ALL filter, search, and sort combinations, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display only Lead records that satisfy every active filter condition simultaneously.
12. WHEN an Operator applies the same filter, search, and sort combination twice with no intervening data change, THE Lead_List_View SHALL display an identical result set in an identical order.
13. IF no Lead record matches the active filter and search combination, THEN THE Lead_List_View SHALL display a zero-match message, SHALL display a match count of 0, and SHALL offer a control that clears every active filter and the search term.
14. IF an Operator requests a page number greater than the total page count for the active filter and search combination, THEN THE Lead_List_View SHALL display the last available page and SHALL retain the active filters, search term, and sort selection.
15. IF the Lead_List_View cannot retrieve Lead or Deal records, THEN THE Lead_List_View SHALL display an error message indicating that the lead set could not be loaded, SHALL retain the active filters, search term, and sort selection, and SHALL offer a retry control.

### Requirement 3: Deal Room Detail View

**User Story:** As an operator, I want one screen per deal showing price, invoice, payment, and release status, so that I can run the whole conversation from a single place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Operator opens the Deal_Room_View for an existing Lead, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display, within 2 seconds, the company_name, industry, contact_name, contact_email, contact_phone, owner, researched_score, Pipeline_State, Suggested_Price, agreed_price when a Deal exists with agreed_price set, Site_Project preview_url and review_state when a Site_Project exists, invoice identifier and issued_at when an invoice exists, payment received amount and paid_date when a payment exists, the Payment_Verified_Flag value, and the website release status.
2. WHILE no Release_Authorization exists for the Lead's Deal, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display the website release status as Locked.
3. THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display the activity history for the Lead ordered by entry timestamp with the most recent entry first, SHALL display the timestamp of every entry, SHALL include email rows with sent_at, opened_at, clicked_at, and reply_at values, call rows with attempt_number and outcome, Pipeline_State changes with previous and new Pipeline_State values, and Operator actions, and SHALL paginate the history at 50 entries per page when more than 50 entries exist.
4. THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display every action control defined for the Lead's current Pipeline_State, SHALL enable each control whose preconditions in Requirements 5 through 8 are satisfied, and SHALL display each disabled control together with the unmet precondition that disables it among current Pipeline_State, missing agreed_price, unset Payment_Verified_Flag, Site_Project review_state other than Approved, a Compliance_Guard blocking condition, and insufficient Operator role.
5. WHEN an Operator submits a call record for a Lead, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL store a call row containing the lead_id, an attempt_number equal to 1 for the Lead's first call row and otherwise one greater than the Lead's highest existing attempt_number, the submission timestamp, an outcome value of answered, busy, or no-answer, and notes of at most 2,000 characters, and SHALL display the stored call row in the activity history, where the 2,000-character notes limit is the Deal_Room_View input limit and is deliberately narrower than the 5,000-character `calls` notes storage ceiling stated in Requirement 13.4, and where the attempt_number is assigned by the Deal_Room_View rather than submitted by the Operator and is deliberately narrower in origin than the `calls` attempt_number storage range of 1 through 20 stated in Requirement 13.4.
6. WHEN an Operator submits an edit to the Lead fields contact_name, contact_email, or contact_phone whose value satisfies criterion 8, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL persist the new value and SHALL create an Audit_Entry containing the actor_id, the previous value, the new value, and occurred_at.
7. IF an Operator opens the Deal_Room_View for a lead_id that does not exist, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display a not-found message, SHALL offer a return path to the Lead_List_View, and SHALL create no Lead, Deal, or call records.
8. IF an Operator submits a contact field edit whose contact_name exceeds 100 characters, whose contact_email is not a syntactically valid email address of at most 254 characters, or whose contact_phone is outside the length range of 7 through 20 characters, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL reject the edit, SHALL retain the previously stored value, and SHALL display a message identifying the rejected field and the accepted value range.
9. IF an Operator submits a call record whose outcome is absent from the set answered, busy, no-answer or whose notes exceed 2,000 characters, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL reject the submission, SHALL create no call row, SHALL retain the Operator-entered values in the input controls, and SHALL display a message identifying the rejected field and the accepted values.
10. WHILE a Release_Authorization exists for the Lead's Deal, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display the website release status as Released together with the Deal delivered_date.
11. WHEN an Operator holding the Agent or Admin role submits an edit to the Lead pricing input fields website_condition, urgency, or estimated_page_count whose value satisfies criterion 12, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL apply the same validated field-edit path applied to the contact fields in criterion 6, SHALL persist the new value, SHALL create an Audit_Entry containing the actor_id, the previous value, the new value, and occurred_at, and SHALL display the Suggested_Price recomputed from the persisted values.
12. IF an Operator submits a pricing input edit whose website_condition or urgency value is not an integer from 1 through 5, or whose estimated_page_count value is not an integer from 0 through 200, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL reject the edit, SHALL retain the previously stored value, and SHALL display a message identifying the rejected field and the accepted value range.

### Requirement 4: Pipeline State Machine

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want pipeline stages to advance only along legal paths, so that the dashboard state always reflects a real sales sequence.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL treat the following 17 ordered pairs as the complete and exhaustive set of Legal_Transitions, and SHALL treat every other ordered pair of Pipeline_State values, including every pair whose two values are identical and every pair whose first value is a Terminal_State, as absent from the Legal_Transition set:
   - New_Lead → Contacted, New_Lead → Closed_Lost
   - Contacted → Replied, Contacted → Closed_Lost
   - Replied → Scheduled, Replied → Quoted, Replied → Closed_Lost
   - Scheduled → Quoted, Scheduled → Closed_Lost
   - Quoted → Won, Quoted → Closed_Lost
   - Won → Invoiced, Won → Closed_Lost
   - Invoiced → Paid_Pending_Verification, Invoiced → Closed_Lost
   - Paid_Pending_Verification → Payment_Verified
   - Payment_Verified → Released
2. WHEN an Operator requests a Pipeline_State change that is a Legal_Transition and whose preconditions in Requirements 5 through 8 are satisfied, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL persist the new Pipeline_State and SHALL create exactly one Audit_Entry recording the previous Pipeline_State as before_value and the new Pipeline_State as after_value, both within 2 seconds of receiving the request.
3. IF an Operator requests a Pipeline_State change whose ordered pair of current and requested Pipeline_State values is absent from the Legal_Transition set, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL reject the request, SHALL retain the current Pipeline_State and every associated Deal field unchanged, SHALL display an error message listing the Pipeline_State values that form a Legal_Transition with the current Pipeline_State, and SHALL create one Audit_Entry recording the rejected attempt.
4. IF an Operator requests any Pipeline_State change for a Lead whose Pipeline_State is a Terminal_State, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL evaluate this rejection before every other validation check applied to the resolved Lead, SHALL reject the request, SHALL retain the Terminal_State, SHALL display a message stating that the Lead is in a final state and that no further Pipeline_State change is available, and SHALL create one Audit_Entry recording the rejected attempt.
5. WHEN a Lead record is created, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL assign the Pipeline_State New_Lead as the first entry of that Lead's recorded Pipeline_State history and SHALL reject any request that sets a Pipeline_State other than New_Lead at creation time.
6. FOR ALL sequences of accepted Pipeline_State change requests applied to one Lead, the first Pipeline_State value in the Lead's recorded history SHALL be New_Lead, every consecutive pair of Pipeline_State values in that history SHALL be a member of the Legal_Transition set, no consecutive pair SHALL contain two identical values, and no Pipeline_State value SHALL follow a Terminal_State.
7. FOR ALL Pipeline_State change requests, whether Operator-initiated or reported by the Pipeline_Adapter, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL produce exactly one of the outcomes accepted-and-applied or rejected-and-unchanged, SHALL leave no partially applied Pipeline_State or Audit_Entry when the outcome is rejected-and-unchanged, and FOR ALL sets of two or more requests targeting the same Lead that are received before the first request completes, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL accept at most one request and SHALL reject each remaining request with a message stating that the Lead's Pipeline_State changed since the request was formed.
8. WHEN the Pipeline_Adapter reports a Lead event, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL map the event to a requested Pipeline_State using exactly the following mapping and SHALL then evaluate that request under criteria 3, 4, and 7: prospect replied maps to Replied, payment received maps to Paid_Pending_Verification, and the event types email opened, email clicked, email bounced, unsubscribed, and site generation finished map to no Pipeline_State change.
9. IF the Pipeline_Adapter reports an event whose mapped Pipeline_State does not form a Legal_Transition with the Lead's current Pipeline_State, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL reject the event, SHALL retain the current Pipeline_State, SHALL record the rejected event with the reported event type and the Lead's current Pipeline_State, and SHALL leave unchanged every other Lead and Deal field that the rejected Pipeline_State change would itself have written, where this clause is scoped to the fields of the rejected transition alone and SHALL NOT require discarding a fact that the event itself records under another criterion, and in particular SHALL NOT require discarding the payment amount, paid_date, and payment_received values that Requirement 8.3 records unconditionally for a payment event.
10. IF an Operator submits a Pipeline_State change request whose target Pipeline_State value is absent from the Pipeline_State value set or whose lead_id does not identify an existing Lead record, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL reject the request before evaluating any validation check, including the Terminal_State check of criterion 4 and the Legal_Transition set of criterion 3, SHALL display an error message identifying whether the target state value or the lead_id was invalid, and SHALL create no Pipeline_State change, where parsing the target Pipeline_State value and resolving the lead_id are resolution steps that establish the subject of validation rather than validation checks.
11. IF an Operator requests a Pipeline_State change that is a Legal_Transition but whose preconditions in Requirements 5 through 8 are not satisfied, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL reject the request, SHALL retain the current Pipeline_State, SHALL display each unsatisfied precondition, and SHALL create one Audit_Entry recording the rejected attempt.
12. THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL evaluate every Pipeline_State change request through the following ordered pipeline and SHALL stop at the first step that rejects the request: step 0, resolution, in which the target Pipeline_State value is parsed and the lead_id is resolved to an existing Lead record under criterion 10; step 1, the Terminal_State pre-check of criterion 4; step 2, the Legal_Transition membership check of criterion 3; step 3, the action precondition checks drawn from Requirements 5 through 8 under criterion 11; step 4, the concurrency guard of criterion 7.
13. WHEN the Pipeline_State_Machine accepts a Pipeline_State change for a Lead, THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL increment that Lead's state_version by one within the same transaction that persists the new Pipeline_State, and IF a Pipeline_State change request carries a submitted state_version that differs from the Lead's current state_version, THEN THE Pipeline_State_Machine SHALL reject that request at the concurrency guard step identified as step 4 in criterion 12, SHALL retain the current Pipeline_State and every associated Deal field unchanged, SHALL apply no second Pipeline_State change, and SHALL display the message required by criterion 7 stating that the Lead's Pipeline_State changed since the request was formed.

### Requirement 5: Outreach Actions and Compliance Blocking

**User Story:** As an operator, I want to trigger emails and log calls from the dashboard while being blocked from contacting anyone who has opted out, so that outreach stays compliant.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Operator confirms a send-email action for a Lead for which the Compliance_Guard reports no blocking condition, THE Outreach_Controller SHALL submit an email request to the Pipeline_Adapter carrying the Lead's contact_email, a subject of 1 to 200 characters, and a body of 1 to 10,000 characters, SHALL record exactly one email row containing lead_id, subject, body, outreach_request_id, and sent_at once the Pipeline_Adapter returns a success result, and SHALL request the Contacted Pipeline_State when the Lead's Pipeline_State is New_Lead.
2. THE Outreach_Controller SHALL require an explicit Operator confirmation step that displays the recipient contact_email, the Lead company_name, and the message subject, SHALL submit no email request until that confirmation is given, and SHALL submit no email request and record no email row when the Operator cancels the confirmation step.
3. IF a Lead has unsubscribed set, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL block every prospect-email action for that Lead, SHALL submit no email request to the Pipeline_Adapter, SHALL record no email row, SHALL leave the Lead's Pipeline_State unchanged, and SHALL display a message identifying the unsubscribe condition and the unsubscribe timestamp.
4. IF a Lead has do_not_call set, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL block every call action for that Lead, SHALL record no call row, SHALL leave the Lead's Pipeline_State unchanged, and SHALL display a message identifying the do-not-call condition.
5. IF an Operator requests a call action at a Lead local time earlier than 08:00 or at or later than 20:00, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL block the action, SHALL record no call row, and SHALL display the Lead's current local time and the Calling_Window bounds of 08:00 to 20:00.
6. IF one or more bounce events are recorded against a Lead's current contact_email, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL block every subsequent email action for that Lead, SHALL set the Lead's Manual_Review_Flag, and SHALL display the recorded bounce reason and the bounce timestamp.
7. IF a Lead shares a contact_email with another Lead when both values are compared case-insensitively after leading and trailing whitespace is removed, or shares a contact_phone with another Lead when both values are compared using digits only, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL display a duplicate-contact warning naming the other Lead's company_name and SHALL require one additional Operator confirmation distinct from the confirmation in criterion 2 before any outreach request is submitted.
8. WHEN the Pipeline_Adapter reports an unsubscribe event for a Lead, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL set the Lead's unsubscribed field with the event timestamp, SHALL set the unsubscribed field on the email row identified by the event's email identifier when the event carries that identifier under Requirement 12.2, SHALL otherwise set the unsubscribed field on that Lead's email row with the greatest sent_at value when that Lead has at least one email row, SHALL block all subsequent prospect-email actions for that Lead, and SHALL display the compliance badge on that Lead's row on the next Lead_List_View render.
9. THE Outreach_Controller SHALL generate one outreach_request_id per Operator-confirmed outreach action before the first submission attempt, SHALL make that value unique across all outreach requests, and SHALL reuse that same value unchanged for every retry of that confirmed action.
10. IF the Outreach_Controller receives a submission carrying an outreach_request_id that already has a recorded email row or call row, THEN THE Outreach_Controller SHALL discard the submission, SHALL submit no request to the Pipeline_Adapter, SHALL record no additional row, and SHALL display the existing record with its recorded timestamp.
11. FOR ALL Leads with unsubscribed_at set, the count of prospect-email requests submitted to the Pipeline_Adapter with a Clearance_Timestamp at or after that unsubscribed_at value SHALL be zero.
12. FOR ALL outreach_request_id values, the count of recorded email rows plus call rows carrying that outreach_request_id SHALL be at most one.
13. WHEN an Operator submits a bulk outreach action over a selected set of 1 to 100 Leads, THE Compliance_Guard SHALL evaluate every blocking condition in criteria 3 through 7 independently for each selected Lead, THE Outreach_Controller SHALL submit requests only for the Leads with no blocking condition, and THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL display each blocked Lead together with the blocking condition that applied to it.
14. IF an Operator selects more than 100 Leads for a bulk outreach action, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the action, SHALL submit no outreach request, and SHALL display the maximum selection size of 100 Leads.
15. IF the Compliance_Guard cannot determine a Lead's local timezone, THEN THE Compliance_Guard SHALL block every call action for that Lead, SHALL record no call row, and SHALL display a message indicating that the Lead's local time is unknown.
16. FOR ALL Leads with do_not_call_at set, the count of call requests submitted to the Pipeline_Adapter with a Clearance_Timestamp at or after that do_not_call_at value SHALL be zero.
17. ### Requirement 7: Price Suggestion and Operator Override

**User Story:** As an operator, I want the dashboard to suggest a price within known bounds while I set the final number, so that quotes stay profitable and I keep negotiating room.

1. 2. WHEN an Operator opens the Deal_Room_View for a Lead, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display, in whole US dollars, the Suggested_Price computed from the Lead's current page_count, website_condition, and urgency values, together with the Price_Floor of 550, the Price_Anchor of 850, and the Price_Cap of 1000, alongside the agreed_price input.

THE Deal_Room_View SHALL label the Suggested_Price as a recommendation, SHALL leave the agreed_price input empty until an Operator submits a value, and SHALL reject a quote action or a create-invoice action for a Deal whose agreed_price has not been set by an Operator submission while displaying a message requiring an Operator-entered agreed_price.
4. IF an Operator submits an agreed_price below the Price_Floor of 550 or above the Price_Cap of 1000, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL reject the value, SHALL display the accepted range of 550 to 1000 whole US dollars, SHALL retain the previously persisted agreed_price or leave the agreed_price unset when no value was previously persisted, and SHALL leave the Lead's Pipeline_State unchanged.

WHEN an Operator submits an agreed_price that is greater than or equal to 550 and less than or equal to 1000, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL persist the submitted value, SHALL display the persisted value within 1 second, and SHALL create an Audit_Entry recording the submitting operator_id, the Suggested_Price at submission time, the previous agreed_price, and the submitted agreed_price.

7. FOR ALL combinations of integer page_count in the range 0 through 200, integer website_condition in the range 1 through 5, and integer urgency in the range 1 through 5, the Suggested_Price SHALL be an integer greater than or equal to 550 and less than or equal to 1000.
8. FOR ALL Deal records, the agreed_price SHALL have been set by an Operator-submitted value rather than by an automatic assignment, and no Suggested_Price computation SHALL write the agreed_price field.
9. IF an Operator submits an agreed_price value that is blank, non-numeric, or not a whole number of US dollars, THEN THE Deal_Room_View SHALL reject the submission, SHALL display a message stating that a whole-dollar amount between 550 and 1000 is required, and SHALL retain the previously persisted agreed_price or leave the agreed_price unset when no value was previously persisted.
10. IF any one or more of a Lead's resolved page_count, website_condition, and urgency inputs is absent when the Suggested_Price is requested, THEN THE Pricing_Advisor SHALL return the Price_Anchor of 850 as the Suggested_Price flagged as a fallback value, SHALL substitute no default value for any individual absent attribute, SHALL evaluate the formula of criterion 1 for no such Lead, and THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display an indicator naming each absent attribute.
11. WHILE an invoice record exists for a Deal, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display the agreed_price input in a disabled state with the reason that the amount is fixed by the issued invoice, and SHALL reject any agreed_price change request for that Deal while retaining the persisted agreed_price.
12. THE Pricing_Advisor SHALL resolve the page_count input for a Lead in the following order: the page_count of the Lead's most recent Site_Project as defined in Requirement 6.11, being that Lead's Site_Project holding the greatest created_at value, when a Site_Project exists for that Lead, otherwise the Lead's estimated_page_count when that value is set, otherwise absent; and SHALL resolve the website_condition and urgency inputs from the Lead's website_condition and urgency values respectively, treating an unset value as absent.
13. THE Pricing_Advisor SHALL exclude the Lead's preferred_price from every Suggested_Price computation, THE Deal_Room_View SHALL display preferred_price in read-only form as a research hint, and no Deal_Room_Dashboard component SHALL copy preferred_price into a Deal agreed_price.

12. THE Analytics_View SHALL display the total revenue computed as the sum of the payment amounts of Deals whose Payment_Verified_Flag is set with a verification timestamp inside the selected date range, the count of invoices with issued_at inside the selected date range, the count of those invoices with paid_date set, and the median whole number of days between issued_at and paid_date over those invoices with paid_date set, where the median of an even-sized set is the arithmetic mean of the two central values and a set with no paid invoices is displayed as not-applicable.

14. WHEN an Operator opens or recomputes the Analytics_View over a data set of 5,000 Lead records or fewer and 50,000 email rows or fewer, THE Analytics_View SHALL display every metric within 3 seconds, and IF no records fall inside the selected date range, THEN THE Analytics_View SHALL display every count as zero and every rate metric as not-applicable.
15. FOR ALL Cohorts and FOR ALL selected date ranges, each Cohort_Stage_Count in the ordered stage sequence New_Lead, Contacted, Replied, Scheduled, Quoted, Won, and Released SHALL be less than or equal to the Cohort_Stage_Count of the stage that immediately precedes it in that sequence, every drop-off count displayed under criterion 2 SHALL be greater than or equal to zero, and every drop-off percentage displayed under criterion 2 SHALL be greater than or equal to 0 and less than or equal to 1, and THE Analytics_View SHALL hold these bounds without clamping, because every Lead's recorded Pipeline_State history begins at New_Lead under Requirement 4.5 and the ordered stages are traversed in the forward direction only.

THE Audit_Logger SHALL store Audit_Entry records in append-only form, and IF a request to modify or delete an existing Audit_Entry reaches the Deal_Room_Dashboard, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the request, SHALL leave every field of the existing Audit_Entry unchanged, and SHALL display a message indicating that audit records are immutable.
5. WHEN an Operator opens the audit history for a Lead, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL display the Audit_Entry records whose target_id resolves to that Lead or to that Lead's Deal, ordered by occurred_at descending with entries sharing an occurred_at value ordered by their append sequence, SHALL display actor_id, action_type, before_value, after_value, and occurred_at for each entry, SHALL paginate at 50 entries per page, and SHALL display a no-recorded-activity message when no Audit_Entry exists for that Lead.
6. WHERE an Operator holds the Admin role, THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL display a searchable audit log filterable by actor_id, action_type, and date range, SHALL apply all supplied filter values conjunctively, SHALL order results by occurred_at descending, SHALL paginate at 50 entries per page with the total match count, and SHALL return the first page within 3 seconds for an audit log of 100,000 entries or fewer.
7. IF an Operator who does not hold the Admin role requests the searchable audit log, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the request and SHALL display an authorization-failure message.
8. THE Audit_Logger SHALL retain each Audit_Entry for at least 24 months measured from its occurred_at value, SHALL not delete an Audit_Entry within that period, and SHALL make every Audit_Entry within that period available to the views described in criteria 5 and 6.
9. IF the Audit_Entry write for an Operator action fails, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL reject the Operator action, SHALL leave the target Lead, Deal, Site_Project, invoice, payment, and Release_Authorization records at their pre-action values, and SHALL display a message indicating that the action was not recorded and was not applied.
10. FOR ALL applied Operator actions and all rejected action attempts covered by criterion 3, exactly one corresponding Audit_Entry SHALL exist, and a submission discarded as a duplicate SHALL add no further Audit_Entry beyond the one recorded for the original submission.

### Requirement 12: Pipeline Adapter Boundary

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the dashboard to talk to the automation through one adapter with stub implementations, so that the dashboard ships and works before the bot exists.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL expose exactly the five outbound operations generate site preview, send prospect email, send delivery email, create invoice, and log outbound call, SHALL return for each invocation exactly one result of success or failure, where a failure result carries a failure reason of 1 to 500 characters, and SHALL require every invocation of each of those five outbound operations to carry an Idempotency_Key that is generated once per Operator-confirmed action and is stable across every retry of that same confirmed action, where the outreach_request_id of Requirement 5.9 is the Idempotency_Key for the send prospect email and log outbound call operations.
2. THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL expose an inbound event intake that accepts exactly the seven event types email opened, email clicked, prospect replied, email bounced, unsubscribed, payment received, and site generation finished, and SHALL require on every inbound event an event identifier of 1 to 128 characters, an event type, a lead_id, and an event timestamp, SHALL additionally require a deal_id and a payment amount on a payment received event, and SHALL accept on an unsubscribed event an optional email identifier naming the email row the unsubscribe originated from, where an unsubscribed event that omits that email identifier remains a valid event.
3. WHERE the Pipeline_Adapter is configured with a stub implementation, WHEN an Operator-confirmed action invokes a Pipeline_Adapter operation, THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL record the operation name with every submitted argument, SHALL return a success result within 1 second, SHALL transmit no message to any recipient outside the Deal_Room_Dashboard, and THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL display a stub-mode indicator on every action control that invokes a Pipeline_Adapter operation.
4. IF a Pipeline_Adapter operation returns a failure result, THEN THE Deal_Room_Dashboard SHALL retain the Lead's current Pipeline_State, SHALL leave the Lead, Deal, email, call, invoice, and payment records unchanged, SHALL display the failure reason returned by the operation, and SHALL offer a retry control that resubmits the same operation with the same Idempotency_Key.
5. WHEN the Pipeline_Adapter receives an inbound event whose event identifier matches an event identifier processed within the preceding 90 days, THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL discard the event and SHALL leave all Lead, Deal, email, call, Site_Project, invoice, and payment records unchanged, and THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL retain each processed event identifier for at least 90 days.
6. IF the Pipeline_Adapter receives an inbound event that omits any field required by criterion 2, or whose event timestamp is not a valid date and time value, or whose lead_id does not identify an existing Lead record, THEN THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL reject the event, SHALL record the rejected payload together with the rejection reason, SHALL leave all Lead, Deal, email, call, Site_Project, invoice, and payment records unchanged, and SHALL apply no Pipeline_State change.
7. FOR ALL inbound events and FOR ALL repeat delivery counts N from 1 through 10, the stored Lead, Deal, email, call, Site_Project, invoice, payment, and Pipeline_State values after delivering one event N times SHALL equal the values after delivering that event exactly once, excluding Audit_Entry records and duplicate-discard records.
8. IF a Pipeline_Adapter operation does not return a result within 30 seconds of invocation, THEN THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL return a failure result whose reason indicates a timeout and SHALL record no email, call, invoice, or Release_Authorization record for that invocation.
9. IF the Pipeline_Adapter receives an inbound event whose event type is absent from the seven event types listed in criterion 2, THEN THE Pipeline_Adapter SHALL reject the event, SHALL record the rejected payload together with the rejection reason, and SHALL leave all Lead, Deal, email, call, Site_Project, invoice, and payment records unchanged.

## Out of Scope

Post-payment refund, chargeback, and cancellation handling is deliberately not specified in this document. Requirement 4.1's transition table therefore contains no edge leaving Paid_Pending_Verification, Payment_Verified, or Released other than the forward edges listed there, and no refunded Pipeline_State value exists. A follow-up specification is required before the Deal_Room_Dashboard can represent a payment dispute; it must settle whether a refund revokes a delivered website, whether it reverses the revenue figure of Requirement 10.12, and whether it requires a second approving Operator.
